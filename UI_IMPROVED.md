**Before:** Cramped icons, hard to read, overwhelming
**After:** Clean, spacious, modern design

#### Desktop (≥768px):
- ✅ Full labels visible: "News", "Lyrics", "Flashcards", "Progress", "Settings"
- ✅ Proper spacing between items
